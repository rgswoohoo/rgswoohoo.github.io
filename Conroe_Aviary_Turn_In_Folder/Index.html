A Home For Exotic Birds

Here at Conroe Aviary, we take care of some of the planets rarer birds and raise some for bird lovers to own as pets in the future. There are only so many types of birds we are allowed to own in the US. Some just make better pets and thus are more popular. We try to focus more on these popular birds to have them available for the demand. We have different types of parrots, love birds, cockatoos, cockatiels, European Starlings! We also take in and rescue birds so that we can provide safe shelter for them to live in with a large, enclosed area for them to fly around.

The caged-in area keeps them from flying into the wild (where they probably would not survive) and keeps anything from getting in to harm them. Birds sometimes come to us malnourished and in bad health, so we get the opportunity to work with them and nourish them back to good health! Thus, we also act as a rescue.

Since we are an aviary, we are open to the public. We welcome everyone to come in and interact with the birds in the aviary – whether you are interested in taking one home or you just like to come and visit for a bit. You’ll enjoy the atmosphere of chirps, squawks, and squeaks!

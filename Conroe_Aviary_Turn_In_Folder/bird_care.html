Caring for your birds!

All our birds are a type or species of parrot. They are all used to warmer climates so this must be taken into consideration during winter months. They are friendly birds who show affection and like to spend time with or around their owners. Most like to cuddle and be pet. They can be excitable, so you will need to be cautious if you introduce them to younger children. They eat seeds, sunflower specifically, and dried fruits. Give them room to fly, but make sure they do not escape!
